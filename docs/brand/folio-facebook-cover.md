## Generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: finished Facebook Page cover image for Folio, an online PDF, invoice and image tool website.
Create a premium, beautifully typeset wide horizontal brand cover, approximately 1702 x 630 pixels, aspect ratio 2.70:1. This is the finished flat artwork, not a mockup of a Facebook interface.
The attached image is a reference ONLY for the Folio logo: preserve its distinctive geometric F mark, lowercase "folio." wordmark and square accent. On the dark background render the main logo in warm white, with the tiny accent strokes/dot in orange. Do not retain the reference's white rectangular background.
Design: matte charcoal #191919 across the full canvas, subtle tonal depth, polished editorial visual style matching a modern clean website. Primary accent vivid warm orange #ff773d. Bold, clean Geist-like sans serif typography with tight but readable spacing. Restrained colors, generous clear space, no clutter.
Composition: center all essential branding and copy in the middle 58% of the canvas width and middle 72% of its height. Top and bottom margins generous. Keep both outer edges decorative and the bottom-left area especially quiet so a profile-photo overlay does not obscure any information. Essential words must remain intact with substantial side cropping on phones.
Central lockup, exact text:
Small white/orange Folio logo at top of central lockup.
Main headline in two large confident lines:
"Less paperwork."
"More possibilities."
First line white; second line orange.
Smaller white-gray service line underneath:
"PDF tools · Invoices · Images"
Website line in crisp white beneath with generous separation:
"thebestfreepdf.com"
Keep all lettering sharp, perfectly spelled, easily readable, and optically aligned. No other words.
Supporting visuals: at the extreme left, a tasteful small arrangement of tactile off-white paper/document corners with minimal printed gray rules, gently tilted and partly out of frame; at extreme right, a refined sculptural stack of ivory document sheets with an orange accent tab and a simple orange image-outline pictogram on the front card. Subtle real soft shadows and dimensionality. The paper objects should be secondary to the strong centered typography, occupy no more than the outer 20% on either side, and never intrude into the central text. No readable extra text, financial numbers, busy UI, stock-photo people, watermarks, gradients in the type, blue or purple, feature-count claims, free-for-everything claims, or Facebook branding.
